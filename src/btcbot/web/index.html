How it works

1 · DataEach day after the 00:00 UTC close, the bot pulls daily BTC-USD candles from Coinbase's public API.
2 · Features17 lookahead-free signals: momentum, volatility, trend, RSI, MACD, volume and drawdown. A test enforces that none of them use future data.
3 · ModelA gradient-boosted classifier predicts P(price up tomorrow). It retrains every Sunday with embargoed walk-forward validation.
4 · TradeThe bot buys when P(up) ≥ 0.55 and sells below 0.50, paying 0.15% per trade. The results are committed to the repo and published here.
